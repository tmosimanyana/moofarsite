Our History

Founded in 2025 by Mooketsi Mapugwa, Moofar Proprietary Limited began with a vision to redefine landscaping standards across Botswana. Over time, the company has expanded from a small local operation into a trusted brand serving residential, commercial, and government clients nationwide.

Managed by Farai Madorobo, our experienced team focuses on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. We believe in blending nature and design to create spaces that inspire and endure — delivering excellence in every garden, yard, and outdoor project we touch.
