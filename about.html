About Moofar

Our Story & Values

Our Journey

Cultivating Excellence Since 2010

Moofar Pty Ltd was founded with a simple vision: to transform Botswana's landscapes with expertise, creativity, and unwavering commitment to quality. What began as a small family operation has grown into one of the country's most respected landscaping and horticultural service providers.

Over the years, we've completed more than ten projects across residential, commercial, and agricultural sectors. From intimate garden designs to large-scale land clearing operations, our dedication to excellence remains constant. We've built our reputation on the quality of our work, the expertise of our team, and the trust of our clients.

Today, Moofar stands as a testament to what passion, hard work, and attention to detail can achieve. We continue to evolve, adopting sustainable practices and modern techniques while staying true to the values that built our foundation.
