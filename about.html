About Moofar Pty Ltd

Our Story

At Moofar Pty Ltd, we are passionate about transforming outdoor spaces across Botswana. With over 15 years of expertise, we deliver high-quality landscaping, bush clearing, fencing, and hardscaping services that blend creativity, craftsmanship, and care.

Founded in Gaborone, Moofar Pty Ltd began as a small, family-driven landscaping company. Our dedication to excellence and client satisfaction has grown us into one of Botswana's most reputable landscaping businesses, serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients with professionalism and integrity.

Our History

Founded in Gaborone over 15 years ago, Moofar Pty Ltd started with a simple vision: to bring beauty and functionality to outdoor spaces throughout Botswana. What began as a small family operation has evolved into a trusted name in landscaping, known for our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Throughout our journey, we've completed over 500 successful projects ranging from residential gardens to large commercial developments. Our growth reflects not just our business success, but the trust our clients place in us to transform their outdoor visions into reality.

Transform Your Space

Every outdoor space holds potential. Whether it's a garden, business complex, or farm, our mission is to bring your vision to life with creativity and quality workmanship. We don't just design landscapes — we cultivate experiences that grow and thrive.

Our approach combines sustainable practices with innovative design solutions. We carefully select native and adapted plant species, implement efficient irrigation systems, and use eco-friendly materials whenever possible. This ensures that your landscape not only looks beautiful today but remains vibrant and sustainable for years to come.

Our Values

Excellence: We maintain the highest standards in every project, from initial consultation to final installation and ongoing maintenance.

Integrity: We build lasting relationships with our clients through honest communication, transparent pricing, and reliable service.

Sustainability: We're committed to environmentally responsible practices that protect Botswana's natural beauty while creating stunning outdoor spaces.

Innovation: We stay current with the latest landscaping techniques, materials, and equipment to provide you with cutting-edge solutions.
