Moofar Proprietary Limited

We transform outdoor spaces into beautiful, functional environments with expert landscaping, maintenance, and horticultural care throughout Botswana.

History

Officially incorporated in September 2025, Moofar Proprietary Limited was founded by Mooketsi Mapugwa with a clear vision: to provide comprehensive, high-quality landscaping solutions throughout Botswana. What started as a passion for transforming outdoor spaces has grown into a full-service company serving residential and commercial clients nationwide.

Under the skilled management of Farai Madorobo, we've built a reputation for reliability, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction. Our six core services are designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients.
