About Moofar Pty Ltd

Our Story

At Moofar Pty Ltd, we’re passionate about transforming outdoor spaces across Botswana. With over 15 years of expertise, we deliver high-quality landscaping, bush clearing, fencing, and hardscaping services that blend creativity, craftsmanship, and care.

Founded in Gaborone, Moofar began as a small, family-driven landscaping company. Through dedication, professionalism, and customer trust, we’ve grown into one of Botswana’s most reputable landscaping service providers — serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients nationwide.

Our History

From humble beginnings in Francistown, Moofar Pty Ltd started with a clear mission: to bring beauty, order, and sustainability to outdoor environments across Botswana. What began as a local family operation has evolved into a trusted national brand known for reliability, innovation, and quality.

We’ve completed over 500 successful projects, ranging from home gardens to large-scale commercial developments — a reflection of the confidence our clients place in our expertise and service excellence.

Transforming Spaces with Purpose

We believe every outdoor space holds untapped potential. Whether it’s a residential garden, a corporate property, or agricultural land — our goal is to help you bring that vision to life. We don’t just build landscapes; we cultivate experiences that grow and thrive.

Our approach combines creative design, sustainable practices, and the use of locally adapted plant species to ensure your outdoor spaces remain vibrant and eco-friendly for years to come.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence: We maintain the highest standards from concept to completion and beyond.
  • Integrity: We value transparency, honesty, and long-term relationships built on trust.
  • Sustainability: We protect Botswana’s natural beauty through environmentally conscious methods.
  • Innovation: We continually embrace modern landscaping techniques and technologies.
