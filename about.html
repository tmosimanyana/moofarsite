Moofar Pty Ltd

At Moofar Pty Ltd, we are passionate about transforming outdoor spaces across Botswana. With over 15 years of expertise, we deliver high-quality landscaping, bush clearing, fencing, and hardscaping services that blend creativity, craftsmanship, and care.

History

Founded in Gaborone, Moofar Pty Ltd began as a small, family-driven landscaping company. Our dedication to excellence and client satisfaction has grown us into one of Botswana’s most reputable landscaping businesses, serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients with professionalism and integrity.

Transform Your Space

Every outdoor space holds potential. Whether it’s a garden, business complex, or farm, our mission is to bring your vision to life with creativity and quality workmanship. We don’t just design landscapes — we cultivate experiences that grow and thrive.
