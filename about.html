Professional Landscaping Services

We specialize in professional Landscaping, Bush clearing, Gardening, Fencing, Maintenance, and Horticultural Nursery services across Botswana. Our experienced team delivers quality workmanship that transforms outdoor spaces and enhances property value.

History

Officially incorporated in September 2025, Moofar Proprietary Limited was founded by Mooketsi Mapugwa with a clear vision: to provide comprehensive, high-quality landscaping solutions throughout Botswana. What started as a passion for transforming outdoor spaces has grown into a full-service company serving residential and commercial clients nationwide.

Under the skilled management of Farai Madorobo, we've built a reputation for reliability, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction. Our six core services are designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques and equipment to deliver outstanding results that stand the test of time. Whether it's a residential garden makeover, commercial property maintenance, land clearing for development, or providing quality plants from our nursery, we approach every project with professionalism and care.
