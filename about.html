Moofar Landscape Services

We design, install, and maintain sustainable landscapes that enhance properties and connect people to place. From native plant meadows to custom hardscape, our team blends craftsmanship with ecological restoration.

History

Founded by a team of landscape designers and restoration specialists, Moofar started with the goal of creating resilient landscapes that thrive with minimal maintenance and support local biodiversity.

Over the years we've completed residential gardens, public green spaces, and ecological restorations—always prioritizing native plants, water-wise design, and long-term site health.
