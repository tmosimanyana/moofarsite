Moofar Proprietary Limited

We specialize in professional landscaping, bush clearing, and fencing services across Botswana. Our experienced team delivers quality workmanship that transforms outdoor spaces and enhances property value. From residential gardens to commercial properties, we bring expertise and dedication to every project.

Our Story

Officially incorporated in September 2025, Moofar Proprietary Limited was founded by Mooketsi Mapugwa with a vision to provide comprehensive landscaping solutions throughout Botswana. Under the management of Farai Madorobo, we've built a reputation for reliability and quality.

Our core services—landscaping, bush clearing, and fencing—are designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques to deliver outstanding results that stand the test of time.
