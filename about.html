Our Story

Moofar Pty Ltd transforms outdoor spaces across Botswana. With over 15 years of expertise, we deliver high-quality landscaping, bush clearing, fencing, and hardscaping services.

Founded in Gaborone, Moofar began as a small family business and grew to serve residential, commercial, and industrial clients with integrity and professionalism.
