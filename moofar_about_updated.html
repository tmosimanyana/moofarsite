About Moofar Proprietary Limited

Transforming Botswana's outdoor spaces with excellence and expertise

Who We Are

Moofar Proprietary Limited (UIN: BW00009410484) is a professional landscaping and horticultural services company operating throughout Botswana. We specialize in comprehensive outdoor property solutions for both residential and commercial clients, delivering quality workmanship and reliable service on every project.

From landscape design to ongoing maintenance, from bush clearing to nursery services, we are your complete outdoor property partner across Botswana.

Our History

Founded in September 2025, Moofar Proprietary Limited began with a clear vision: to bring professional, reliable landscaping services to every corner of Botswana. Under the leadership of founder and director Mooketsi Mapugwa, the company quickly established itself as a trusted name in the industry.

What started as a commitment to quality has grown into a comprehensive service offering that covers everything from initial land preparation to ongoing garden maintenance. Our approach combines traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques, ensuring every project meets the highest standards.

Today, we serve clients across all regions of Botswana, from Gaborone to Maun, bringing the same dedication and expertise to every project, regardless of size or scope. Our team of skilled professionals takes pride in creating outdoor spaces that enhance property value and bring joy to our clients.
